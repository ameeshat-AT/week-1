Calender

A calendar year is divided into 12 distinct months, which help us organize 365 or 366 days into manageable periods.

January & February

The coldest months in the Northern Hemisphere, marking the start of the year.

March, April & May

A period of spring characterized by blooming flowers and warming weather.

June, July & August:

The warmest summer months, known for longer days and summer holidays.

September, October, November & December:

The autumn and early winter months, which close out the year with harvesting, seasonal festivals, and celebrations.

These months range from 28 to 31 days in length and are deeply connected to the Earth's orbit around the Sun, marking the continuous cycle of seasonal changes throughout the year.
