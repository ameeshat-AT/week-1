Vanila Sponge Cake

INGREDIANTS

  • maida:200g
  • Baking powder 1.5 tspn
  • Salt 1/4 tspn
  • Unsalted 115g
  • Granulated sugar 175g
  • Large eggs 2 number
  • Vanilla extract
  • Whole milk 120 ml
  • Optional substitute
    • baking soda +1 teaspoon lemon juice or vinegar for Baking powder
    • vegetable oil or salted butter for unsaltted butter
    • brown sugar instead of granulated sugar
    • yogurt or mashed banana for eggless version
    • maple syrup or almond extract instead of vanila extract

Method steps

  1. Prep Work: Preheat your oven to 170°C (340°F). Grease an 8-inch round cake pan with butter or oil, and line the bottom with parchment paper.
  2. Whisk Dry Ingredients: In a medium bowl, sift together the flour, baking powder, and salt. Set this mixture aside.
  3. Cream Butter and Sugar: In a large bowl, beat the softened butter and sugar together using an electric mixer or whisk. Mix for 2 to 3 minutes until the texture becomes pale, light, and fluffy
  4. Add Wet Ingredients: Add the eggs one at a time, beating well after each addition to keep the batter smooth. Stir in the vanilla extract.
  5. Combine Batter: Alternate adding the dry ingredients and the milk into your wet mixture in 3 separate batches (start and end with the flour). Mix gently until just combined; overmixing will make the cake dense
  6. Bake:Pour the smooth batter into your prepared pan. Tap the pan gently on the counter to remove trapped air bubbles. Bake for 35 to 40 minutes. The cake is ready when a toothpick inserted into the center comes out completely clean.
  7. Cool and Serve:Let the cake cool in the pan for 10 minutes, then flip it out onto a wire cooling rack to cool completely before slicing or frosting

Cooking term glossary

Preheat:
Heating your oven to the required temperature before putting the food inside.
sift:
Passing dry ingredients through a fine mesh sieve to break up lumps and add air.
Creaming::
Beating fat (butter) and sugar together until light, airy, and pale in color.
